1. Start from fresh local data. Open the map, set two prefectures to different levels, close and reopen the app, and confirm the score and levels persist.
2. Change a level from Flags and from prefecture detail. Search, filter by region and level, and check empty results.

4. Export a PNG with flags hidden and shown. Check exact dimensions, visible map/labels/flags, and the optional display name. Cancel the share sheet and confirm that no failure is shown.
5. Switch among English, Japanese, and Traditional Chinese; inspect small and tablet layouts, safe areas, large text, and screen-reader labels.
6. Reset progress, verify its confirmation copy, and confirm all 47 levels return to zero after relaunch.
