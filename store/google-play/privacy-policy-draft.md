# JapanEx Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 25, 2026**

JapanEx is an offline-first application for recording your experiences across Japan's 47 prefectures. This policy explains how the application handles information.

## Information stored on your device

JapanEx stores your prefecture experience levels, selected language, and optional display name locally on your device. This information is used to display your map, calculate your Japan score, and create result images. If device backup is enabled, the operating system may include the local database in a backup. The publisher does not operate a sync or backup server and does not receive this locally stored information through the app.

**Settings → Reset progress** clears prefecture levels but leaves the optional display name and selected language. To remove all locally stored information, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Device backups may retain a copy until removed through the device or backup-provider settings.

## Information you choose to share

JapanEx can create a result image or a view-only link when you request it. A view-only link contains your prefecture experience levels, selected language, and optional display name in the link itself. If you choose to copy, share, or open a link, the services and recipients you select may process that information under their own privacy policies.

JapanEx uses the operating system's sharing tools. The publisher does not store shared result images or view-only link contents in an application database. Opening a view-only link sends the requested URL to the hosting infrastructure needed to deliver the web page. That infrastructure may process standard request information, such as an IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the page. Confirm the hosting provider's specific practices before publishing this policy.

## Device language

JapanEx reads the device's language setting to select an initial interface language. This setting is processed on your device and is not sent to the publisher.

## Accounts, advertising, and analytics

JapanEx does not require an account. The application does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs and does not sell personal information.

## Children's privacy

JapanEx is not directed specifically to children. The publisher does not knowingly receive personal information from children or other users through the application.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if JapanEx's features or information practices change. The updated policy will be published at the same URL with a revised "Last updated" date.
